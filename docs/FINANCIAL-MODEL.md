# Financial model — planning scenarios only

Proposed Pro: $7.99 monthly / $39.99 annually. Preserve current price IDs until corresponding test prices are verified. No live billing authorized. Actual revenue, invoices, expenses, paid demand and subscriber counts are unknown; observed zero subscription rows are not a full accounting audit.

Assumptions: 70% annual subscribers, 30% monthly; 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction plus 0.7% billing allowance; these are editable planning inputs, not verified account-specific Stripe fees. Annual fees normalized over 12 months. Variable data $0.25 and support $0.50 per subscriber monthly. Replacement acquisition assumes 3% monthly churn and $20 CAC ($0.60/subscriber/month). Refund reserve 2% revenue. Initial acquisition expenditure $20 per subscriber is shown separately and excluded from recurring contribution. No owner salary, taxes, disputes, international fees or licensed-data contract minimums included.

MRR equivalent = annual subscribers × annual price / 12 + monthly subscribers × monthly price. ARR equivalent = 12 × normalized MRR, not cash collected or recognized accounting revenue. Operating contribution = revenue − payment/billing fees − infrastructure/data − support − replacement ads − refunds. Net recurring revenue after refunds = revenue × 0.98. Contribution margin = operating contribution / revenue.

Sensitivity: each extra $1 in monthly data/support/CAC allocation reduces contribution by the subscriber count in dollars. Each additional 1 percentage point monthly churn at assumed $20 CAC adds $0.20/subscriber/month in replacement acquisition. Fantasy football demand is seasonal; steady-state churn assumptions can understate off-season cancellations. No model scenario establishes profitability.
